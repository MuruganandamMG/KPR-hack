# Chennai civic complaint ownership: source review

Research snapshot: 15 October 2026. Scope: Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) limits, with named external agencies where GCC's official material assigns the service elsewhere. This is a responsibility map, not a guarantee that GCC owns every asset or street; check asset and jurisdiction when uncertain.

## Main finding

The current classifier's six labels are useful **issue categories**, but most corresponding department names are not GCC department names. GCC's own department directory names Electrical, Solid Waste Management, Storm Water Drain, Health, Roads/Bus Route Roads, Bridges, Parks, Buildings, Town Planning and Revenue, among others. It describes General Administration as personnel and administrative matters, rather than a general public-complaint owner. See [GCC department directory](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/about-GCC/greater-chennai-corporation/departments/) and [department index](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/).

Treat category (what is wrong) separately from owner (which authority has the duty). For multiple issues, keep one dominant category if the contract requires it, but name only the actual owner(s) supported by the complaint. If asset ownership or the responsible authority is unclear, mark for officer review instead of assigning a generic GCC office.

## Current six categories

| Current category | Source-backed owner and boundary | Practical classifier guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Water Supply** | Household water supply, water connections, service operation and maintenance belong to the **Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (CMWSSB / Chennai Metro Water)**, a separate board. Its citizen charter accepts complaints about operation and maintenance of water supply and sewerage disposal. [CMWSSB citizen charter](https://cmwssb-dev.interlaceindia.com/cmwssb_cms_gigw/citizencharter) · [Tamil Nadu State Finance Commission report](https://www.tnsfc.tn.gov.in/assets/pdf/reports/en/sixth/SixthStateFin-MainReport-TEXT-English.pdf) | Do not call the owner a GCC “Water Supply Department.” Keep the service category, identify CMWSSB as the agency, and refer unresolved boundary cases for review. GCC's Storm Water Drain department owns rainwater drainage, which is a different service. |
| **Roads** | GCC's **Bus Route Roads / Roads** and zonal engineering teams maintain GCC interior and bus-route roads, including road resurfacing and pothole/patch work. GCC says service departments such as CMWSSB and the electricity utility cut roads, while GCC handles restoration/road maintenance. [GCC Roads](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/road/) · [GCC road maintenance procedure](https://portal.chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/rules-and-procedure/road-maintenance/) | Replace the synthetic owner **Public Works** with GCC Roads/Bus Route Roads when the road is GCC-maintained. Check jurisdiction for highways, roads owned by another public body, or a utility fault that caused the cut. |
| **Sanitation** | This label conflates at least two GCC owners: **Solid Waste Management (SWM)** handles collection/removal of solid waste and conservancy/road cleaning; **Health/Public Health** handles public-health matters, environmental sanitation, food-safety enforcement and health nuisances. [GCC SWM department](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/solid-waste-management/) · [GCC Health department](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/health/) · [GCC complaint contacts](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/complaints/) | Route litter, missed collection, dumping and street sweeping to GCC SWM. Route food adulteration, disease/vector concerns and other public-health nuisances to GCC Health. “Sanitation” alone is too broad to identify one office. |
| **Electricity** | GCC **Electrical Department** installs and maintains municipal streetlights and electrical installations in GCC buildings and public facilities. Power failure, voltage, meter/billing, dangerous poles and conductors are electricity-distribution complaints handled through **TNPDCL/TANGEDCO's** complaint system, a separate utility. [GCC Electrical Department](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/electrical/) · [TNPDCL consumer grievance guidance](https://www.tnebltd.gov.in/cgrfonline/) | Separate non-burning municipal streetlights (GCC Electrical) from a home/street electricity supply fault or dangerous distribution equipment (TNPDCL/TANGEDCO). The category must not imply one owner for both. |
| **Drainage** | GCC **Storm Water Drain (SWD)** builds, maintains and desilts stormwater drains designed to reduce rainwater stagnation/flooding. **CMWSSB** owns water/sewerage services, including sewerage disposal and sewer-network operation. [GCC SWD responsibilities](https://portal.chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/storm-water/) · [CMWSSB citizen charter](https://cmwssb-dev.interlaceindia.com/cmwssb_cms_gigw/citizencharter) | Distinguish rainwater drain blockage/waterlogging (GCC SWD) from sewage/sewer connection/blockage/overflow (CMWSSB). A generic “Drainage Department” will misroute sewer complaints. |
| **Other** | “Other” is a classifier fallback, not a GCC department. GCC General Administration is responsible for Corporation personnel and administration. [GCC department directory](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/about-GCC/greater-chennai-corporation/departments/) | Do not map every unclear, non-civic, or newly recognized issue to **General Administration**. Route a clearly identified civic issue to its functional owner; send uncertain or out-of-scope cases for officer review. |

## Ownership guardrails

- **Stormwater versus sewage:** classify by the reported system, not the word “drain.” Rainwater stagnation and storm drains point to GCC SWD; sewerage points to CMWSSB.
- **Streetlight versus power supply:** non-burning GCC streetlight points to GCC Electrical; household supply, voltage, meter/billing, or distribution-line hazards point to TNPDCL/TANGEDCO. GCC's [Electrical page](https://chennaicorporation.gov.in/gcc/department/electrical/) describes municipal lighting, while the utility's [complaint guidance](https://www.tnebltd.gov.in/cgrfonline/) names grid-service complaint types.
- **Waste versus public health:** garbage collection and street cleaning point to GCC SWM; illness/vector/food-safety/public-health nuisance points to GCC Health. Co-occurring issues may need a secondary owner only when the complaint reports work that owner must perform.
- **Road responsibility is asset-specific:** GCC documents maintenance of bus-route and interior roads, but its own Roads page notes other utility agencies and road cuts. Resolve the road/asset owner when unclear instead of assuming every Chennai road belongs to GCC.
- Official intake exists: GCC's public complaint directory names service contacts and GCC 1913; CMWSSB's charter lists its own water/sewerage complaint system; TNPDCL has a separate distribution complaint channel. These channels confirm separate ownership, not an existing automatic inter-agency handoff.

## Sources

All sources above are official GCC, CMWSSB, TNPDCL/TANGEDCO or Tamil Nadu State Finance Commission pages/reports. Pages may use older department naming or service statistics; this note relies on the stated function/ownership, not volatile counts or contact details.
